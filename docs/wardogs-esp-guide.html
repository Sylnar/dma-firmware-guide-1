Wardogs ESP and DMA overlay guide

Sylnar Wardogs brings second-PC ESP, aimbot, radar, and world awareness to the 100-player tactical shooter from BULKHEAD and Team17. It is available now.

Players, vehicles, and the world

Player visuals include boxes, names, health, distance, skeletons, snap lines, and current weapon. Ground vehicles and helicopters have separate controls and range settings. FOBs, backpacks, tower codes, factions, and practice targets can be filtered to keep the map readable.

Colour distinguishes genuinely visible enemies from hidden contacts. The Sylnar Wardogs ESP page documents the current controls and availability.

Aim, trigger, and artillery

Targeting supports body-part selection, field-of-view limits, smoothing, target lead, recoil control, and trigger conditions. Tap or hold firing, delays, fire rate, cooldowns, hit radius, and range caps are configurable.

World features include mortar positioning from a map ping and incoming-shell landing indicators with time to impact and a line back to the firing gun.

Second-PC requirements

The same second-machine model as Sylnar CS2 applies: a second Windows PC, supported DMA card, Makcu input device, and display or capture path. Nothing is installed on the PC used to play. Review DMA compatibility before buying.
